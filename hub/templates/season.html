Stacks

A stack is a started quarterback and at least one started WR, TE, or RB on the same matchup side in a posted regular-season week, sharing players.nfl_team. The catcher leg is that catalog position. An RB counts, and the points are the lineup slot, rushing included. Bench and IR slots are out. Two or more catchers are one stack: combined points add the quarterback once and each catcher once. Club is the catalog club on {{ stacks.catalog.catalog_as_of }} ({{ stacks.catalog.with_club }} of {{ stacks.catalog.players }} players). A season before that date uses this later club. Share is combined points divided by official points for. Those points are already inside the team total.
