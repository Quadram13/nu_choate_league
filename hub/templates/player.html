Counting

Sums of every counting stat. G is counting-stat rows, not starts and not NFL games played: a bye or other all-zero week has no row. Rostered and free-agent weeks both count. A /G column is that total divided by G, including kickers and defenses. Columns without a /G header use the same division. PA and YA sum the stored weekly figures. PA/G and YA/G are the defense rates. Total TD is rush + rec + pass and leaves out defensive and special-teams scores. 100Ru, 100Rec, 300P, and 2TD count games at 100+ rush yards, 100+ receiving yards, 300+ pass yards, and 2+ total TDs. Red-zone totals are 0 in 2022 and 2023.
