Regular and playoff are posted weeks on a roster of that kind. Consolation weeks and weeks the player was not rostered stay out of this block. Regular plus playoff is every week here, and the two halves are those same weeks. A missing row is zero weeks. The first half is a week at or before the regular-season midpoint. The second half is every later week, playoffs included. The midpoint is half the regular-season length, rounded down, so the extra week of an odd season is in the second half. {% for bound in splits.bounds %}{{ bound.year }} week {{ bound.midpoint }} of {{ bound.regular_weeks }}{% if not loop.last %} · {% endif %}{% endfor %}. Only a posted week is counted, so the live week stays out. Wk is rostered weeks, bench included. PF sums those weeks. PF/G divides PF by Wk. The counting columns sum the counting-stat rows on those same weeks. G in that box is the counting-row count. A zero-point week is a rostered week and has no counting row, so G can be lower than Wk. A /G rate divides by G.

Sums of every counting stat. G is counting-stat rows, not starts and not NFL games played: a bye or other all-zero week has no row. Rostered and free-agent weeks both count. Total TD is rush + rec + pass and leaves out defensive and special-teams scores. 100Ru, 100Rec, 300P, and 2TD count games at 100+ rush yards, 100+ receiving yards, 300+ pass yards, and 2+ total TDs.
