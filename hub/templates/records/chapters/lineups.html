Lineup counting

Most rushing, receiving, and passing yards by one starting lineup in a posted regular-season week. A starter is a lineup slot with started set and a slot other than BN or IR. Yards sum player_week_counting_stats for those players. A starter with no counting row adds zero. The starters cell says how many of those slots had a counting row when the two counts differ. Quarterback rushing yards are in the rushing total. Kickers and defenses stay in the lineup and usually add zero. Playoff and consolation lineups stay off the board, with the other week records. Posted weeks of an in-progress season stay on, because this is a week record. Player career and single-season counting records are the boards earlier on this page. Kicking there is field goals made. Defense is sacks, defensive interceptions, and points or yards allowed per game for a team defense with at least four counting games.
