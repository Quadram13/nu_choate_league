Season counting

Top season sums from finished regular seasons. An in-progress year is left off, so a short defense sample cannot lead a per-game board. The weekly board above is single-game and rostered. These totals use every counting-stat row, free-agent weeks included, so there is no one manager. G is that row count, not starts. Total TD is rush + rec + pass, not defensive or special-teams scores. Milestone games are 100+ rush yards, 100+ receiving yards, 300+ pass yards, and 2+ total TDs. Points-allowed and yards-allowed boards are DEF only, at least 4 counting games, ranked per game because a sum grows with the number of weeks stored.
