Stacks

A stack is a started quarterback and at least one started WR, TE, or RB on the same matchup side in a posted regular-season week, sharing players.nfl_team. The catcher leg is that catalog position. An RB counts, and the points are the lineup slot, rushing included. Bench and IR slots are out. Two or more catchers are one stack: combined points add the quarterback once and each catcher once. Club is the catalog club on {{ stacks.coverage.catalog.catalog_as_of }} ({{ stacks.coverage.catalog.with_club }} of {{ stacks.coverage.catalog.players }} players have a club). A season before that date uses this later club, so teammates who have since split are missed and players who later joined the same club are matched. The boards below are regular season. Playoff and consolation lineups stay off them, with the other week records.
