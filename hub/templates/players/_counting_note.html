Season and career sums from counting-stat rows. G is not starts: byes and all-zero weeks are omitted, and free-agent weeks count. /G divides by G. PA/G and YA/G are the defense rates (the sums are on the player page). Total TD is rush + rec + pass. 100Ru, 100Rec, 300P, and 2TD are milestone games. The full kicking, first-down, and red-zone line is on each player. Career counting is its own page, so a year chip on the season board does not hide it.
