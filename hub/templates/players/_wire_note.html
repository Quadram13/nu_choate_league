FA is posted lineup weeks the player was not rostered, including a week with no stored score. FA VORP sums those weeks, and a missing score counts as 0 minus that week's replacement. Season is the top 15 by FA VORP. Career sums those seasons, so a year spent entirely unowned can bury one spike. Never rostered is a season with zero rostered weeks, ranked by points while unowned. Highest single weeks are on Records. 2026 is posted weeks only.
