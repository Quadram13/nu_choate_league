Share of regular-season starter points by lineup slot. QB, RB, WR, TE, FLEX, K, and DEF together are the PF column. FLEX RB, FLEX WR, FLEX TE, and other split the FLEX column by the player’s catalog position. Other is a FLEX start catalogued outside RB, WR, and TE. Playoff and consolation weeks stay out of the shares. Shares are rounded to 0.1%. In a finished season the PF column matches official points. {{ league_ir }} IR slots are stored league-wide. The IR column counts those slots. Points stored on them are on the cell, outside PF.

Priority ROI is rest-of-season VORP on a completed waiver add, divided by that add’s claim priority. The dump stores priority 0 on a manager’s first claim in the run. The unit is priority + 1, so the first claim costs 1 and the fifth costs 5. A manager’s ratio is the sum of VORP divided by the sum of those units. Seq is the order the league processed the run. It is listed on each add and is left out of the divisor. The ratio uses 2024 and 2025 completed adds that stored a priority. Failed claims have no production row, so they stay out of the ratio. Completed waiver adds with no stored priority stay out of both sides.
