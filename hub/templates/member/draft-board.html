Draft board

Sums of every draft, including the in-progress season. Rest-of-season numbers for that season cover the weeks played. A hit is 8 or more starts and above that round’s VORP. Hit% is hits divided by picks. The career rate pools the picks. It is the ratio of the sums, so it can differ from the average of the seasons. The career row is the database sum of these seasons. Displayed VORP is rounded to 0.01, so adding the rounded seasons can miss the career row by 0.01.
