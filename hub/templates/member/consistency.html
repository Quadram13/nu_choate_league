Consistency

Weekly team totals on posted weeks, including an unpaired playoff bye. SD is the sample standard deviation. CV is SD / mean. Dev on each week is points minus that season’s own mean. Boom is a week at least 1.5× that row’s own mean. Bust is a week at most half of it. Team totals are tight, so boom and bust weeks are rare and CV is the consistency ranking. This is the same volatility definition as the player page. * 2026 is in progress: the live week is left out until the median team total reaches 40.
